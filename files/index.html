Frequently Asked Questions

GuardianGate, explained

How does GuardianGate work?

Parents register authorised collectors. Security scans the learner QR code. The parent receives an approval request and approves or declines it. The learner is released, and a digital audit trail is automatically stored.

Can more than one guardian collect a learner?

Yes — parents can manage multiple authorised collectors.

Does GuardianGate work on mobile phones?

Yes — parents receive notifications and approve collections directly from their smartphones.

Is the platform cloud-based?

Yes — GuardianGate operates securely in the cloud.

Is there a record of every collection?

Yes — every collection is permanently recorded with date, time and authorised collector information.

Does GuardianGate integrate with existing security systems?

Yes — it complements access control, facial recognition, CCTV and visitor management systems.
