Beverage Authentication Platform

QualiBev™

A mobile application that authenticates alcoholic beverages in real time using QR and NFC technologies — combating South Africa's rising counterfeit alcohol crisis by empowering consumers, protecting brands and supporting government enforcement through a secure, scalable verification platform.

Features

  • Real-time authentication
  • Counterfeit alerts
  • Product traceability
  • Retailer verification
  • Reporting dashboards

Industry Benefits

  • Protects brands from counterfeiting
  • Protects consumers from unsafe products
  • Protects government tax revenue

How It Works

A consumer scans a product's QR code or taps its NFC tag using the QualiBev app. The platform verifies the product against its secure database in real time and immediately flags any suspected counterfeit, alerting both the consumer and the brand.
