Learner Safety Platform

GuardiLink™

A cloud-based learner collection management platform that lets schools digitally verify learner collection through secure QR code identification, real-time parent approval and comprehensive audit trails — improving learner safety and modernising school collection procedures while reducing administrative workload.

Features

  • QR code verification
  • Real-time parent approval
  • Guardian management
  • School dashboards
  • Emergency alerts
  • Full audit trails

Benefits

  • Safer learner collection
  • Reduced liability for schools
  • Operational efficiency
  • Improved parent confidence

How It Works

Parents register authorised collectors. Security scans the learner's QR code. The parent receives an approval request and approves or declines it. The learner is released, and a digital audit trail is automatically stored.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can more than one guardian collect a learner?

Yes — parents can manage multiple authorised collectors.

Does GuardianGate work on mobile phones?

Yes — parents receive notifications and approve collections directly from their smartphones.

Is there a record of every collection?

Yes — every collection is permanently recorded with date, time and authorised collector information.

Does GuardiLink integrate with existing security systems?

Yes — it complements access control, facial recognition, CCTV and visitor management systems.
